# NexusPay phone NFC entry mode — v2.0.9

## Change

NexusPay phone NFC transactions now send DE22 `901` (packed BCD `09 01`),
regardless of whether an online PIN block is present. Previously they used the
ordinary contactless mapping `071`.

Phone detection uses the selected Kona application AID `A0000002514025260010`
and its track-2 HCE account parameter. The physical-card `FFFFFF` filler is
excluded. The activity resolves the mode from the selected AID; the common
host builder also resolves it from DE55 tag 84 or 4F for kernel callers.

The common transaction builder covers sale, refund, preauthorization, balance,
cash and advice transactions, including EMI's delegated sale builder. The
actual DE22 is persisted with the original transaction, so its reversal,
void, preauthorization cancellation, completion, batch upload and offline tip
adjustment retain `901`. Receipt/report entry labels recognize it as NFC.

Existing transactions retain the entry mode originally sent to the host.
Physical cards, scanned QR, merchant-generated QR, contact chip and fallback
keep their existing mappings.

## Validation

Regression coverage includes real ISO-8583 packing and loopback TCP exchanges:

- NexusPay phone detection, physical-card filler and unrelated AIDs.
- Ten financial transaction types with and without an online PIN block.
- Timeout `0200`/`0400` entry-mode consistency and persistence after reload.
- Void, preauthorization void, completion, batch and tip-adjust upload.
- Ordinary contactless, chip, scanned QR and fallback mappings.

`testDebugUnitTest` and `assembleDebug` completed successfully: 89 tests passed,
zero failures or errors. Build/test output: `nexuspay-v209-build.log`.
No physical terminal was connected during this change; NFC reader behavior
and acceptance by the bank host require terminal testing.
